## Purchases
Purchases are handled by Apple. To restore a purchase on a new device, open the app and choose Restore Purchases. Refunds are requested from Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). A subscription is cancelled in Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions.
