Chore-Sized Declutter: support

Chore-Sized Declutter does one thing: photograph a messy spot, get one ten-minute task for it.

Contact

Questions, problems or feedback: northlaneapps@gmail.com. You will get a reply within a few days, from the person who made the app.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. To restore a purchase on a new device, open the app and choose Restore Purchases. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. A subscription is cancelled in Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions.
