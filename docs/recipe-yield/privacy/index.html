Recipe Yield: privacy policy

This policy covers the Recipe Yield app for iPhone, published by Brody Richmond. It is written from what the app's own code does, and it changes when the code does.

What the app collects

Nothing leaves your device. The app has no account, no server and no analytics, and stores nothing about you anywhere but your iPhone.

Your recipes

The recipes you paste and the ones you save are kept in the app's own storage on your iPhone. They are never uploaded or shared, and deleting the app deletes them.

Permissions

The app asks for no system permissions. Pasting and copying use the standard iOS clipboard.

Tracking

The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

Purchases

Any purchase is processed by Apple. The app never sees your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you may ask what personal information the app holds about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and opt out of any sale or sharing of it (the app sells and shares nothing, and holds nothing off your device). Write to the address below and you will get an answer within 45 days. You will not be treated differently for asking.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy appear on this page with a new date. Questions: northlaneapps@gmail.com.
